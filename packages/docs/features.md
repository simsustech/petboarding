# Features

Petboarding is the all-in-one platform for managing your pet boarding and daycare facility. From customer self-service to daily operations and business insights — everything you need in one place.

---

## 🧑‍🦱 Customer Self-Service Portal

Your customers manage their own information, reducing your administrative burden.

- **Personal profiles** — Customers enter their details, emergency contacts, and pet information
- **Online bookings** — Customers create boarding reservations with date ranges, time slots, and optional services
- **Daycare management** — Add daycare dates, purchase prepaid subscriptions, and track remaining days
- **Real-time status** — Customers see pending, approved, or rejected status for every booking and daycare date
- **Email notifications** — Automatic emails on booking approval, rejection, standby, or cancellation

![Customer bookings overview](/screenshots/customer-bookings.png)

---

## 🐕 Comprehensive Pet Management

Keep detailed records of every animal in your care.

- **Rich pet profiles** — Species, breed, gender, birth date, weight, color, chip number, and photo
- **Food & medication tracking** — Feeding schedules (times per day, amount, type) and medication notes
- **Vaccination records** — Upload vaccination documents with expiration dates and automatic compliance checking
- **Pet compatibility ratings** — Rate relationships between pets for safe kennel cohabitation
- **Search & filter** — Full-text search across all pets by name, breed, or chip number

---

## 📆 Boarding Management

Everything you need to run boarding operations smoothly.

- **Booking review workflow** — Approve, reject, or place on standby with automated customer emails
- **Day & week agenda views** — Calendar overview of all arrivals, departures, and active stays
- **Automatic pricing** — Cost calculation based on pet category, stay duration, opening time slots, and optional services
- **Double-booking warnings** — Visual indicators when kennels or dates conflict
- **Down payment support** — Require deposits with automatic payment tracking and reminders
- **Cancelation handling** — Configurable cancelation periods and automatic fee calculation

![Employee agenda view](/screenshots/employee-agenda.png)

![Admin booking management](/screenshots/admin-bookings.png)

---

## 📅 Daycare Operations

Streamline your daily daycare routine.

- **Daycare calendar** — Monthly view of all daycare appointments with pet counts per day
- **Prepaid subscriptions** — Sell packages (e.g., 10 days valid for 6 months) with automatic day deduction
- **Bulk operations** — Approve, reject, or standby multiple daycare dates at once
- **Subscription management** — Customers purchase, track, and renew their daycare packages online

---

## 🏠 Kennel Layout

Visual drag-and-drop kennel assignment for every day.

- **Building & kennel hierarchy** — Organize your facility by buildings with multiple kennels per building
- **Capacity management** — Set kennel capacity and see occupancy at a glance
- **Drag-and-drop assignment** — Assign pets to kennels visually for bookings and daycare
- **Vaccination compliance** — See vaccination status badges on every pet chip
- **Printable layout** — Print the kennel layout for physical reference in your facility

![Kennel layout drag-and-drop](/screenshots/employee-kennellayout.png)

---

## 📊 Business Insights

Stay on top of your business with powerful overview tools.

- **Occupancy calendar** — Monthly heatmap showing booking and daycare pet counts per day
- **Color-coded thresholds** — Green, yellow, orange, and red indicators for capacity planning
- **Financial overview** — Track unpaid bookings, payment status, and outstanding invoices
- **Integrated invoicing** — Automatic SlimFact invoice generation with iDEAL payment support

![Occupancy calendar](/screenshots/admin-occupancy.png)

---

## 📢 Communication

Keep customers informed and engaged.

- **Announcements** — Post general, important, priority, or urgent notices on the public homepage
- **Urgent popups** — Critical announcements appear as persistent dialogs
- **Email templates** — Customizable Handlebars templates for every notification type
- **Locale-aware** — Multi-language email templates (English and Dutch)

---

## ⚙️ Flexible Configuration

Tailor Petboarding to your facility's exact needs.

- **Opening times** — Define arrival and departure time slots with day-of-week scheduling
- **Pricing categories** — Set prices per species and pet size with date-based price overrides
- **Optional services** — Offer add-ons like washing, grooming, or veterinary visits
- **Blocked periods** — Mark dates as unavailable for all, bookings only, or daycare only
- **Vacation surcharges** — Add per-day surcharges during holiday periods
- **Holiday awareness** — Built-in holiday calendars for multiple countries

---

## 🔒 Role-Based Access Control

Three distinct access levels keep data secure and workflows focused.

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **Customer** | Own profile, pets, bookings, and daycare dates only |
| **Employee** | View all customers, pets, bookings; manage kennel layout, agenda, and labels |
| **Administrator** | Full system control: approve bookings, manage accounts, configure settings, view finances |

---

## 🖨️ Printing & Labels

Print out physical materials for your facility whenever you need them.

- **Pet labels** — QR-coded pet information cards for kennel doors
- **Booking labels** — Printable booking reference cards
- **Kennel layout print** — Full facility layout for your operations board
- **Day overview print** — Daily arrivals and departures sheet
