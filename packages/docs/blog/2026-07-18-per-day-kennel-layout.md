# Per-Day Kennel Layout for Bookings

A booking no longer locks every pet into the same kennel for its entire stay. You can now move a pet to a different kennel on a specific day, and the booking will pick up that change for just that day. Daycare assignments were already per-day and are unchanged.

## What changed

- Kennel assignments are now per day, not per booking. The original kennel you picked at booking time is still the default.
- Dragging a pet into a kennel on the layout for a specific day changes only that day.
- Dragging a pet to the waitlist on a specific day removes any future per-day changes you made for that pet in that booking; the original kennel takes over again from that day forward.
- The pet stays in the kennel you picked on every subsequent day of the booking, until you move it again on one of those days.
- Editing a booking (adding or removing pets) clears the per-day changes for that booking and reverts to the booking-level assignment.

## How this improves things

Long stays no longer force pets into one kennel the whole time. A pet that needs a quieter spot halfway through its stay, or a different kennel for a specific event, can be moved on the exact day that matters without affecting the rest of the booking. The layout stays readable: every day shows the kennels that are actually in use on that day.
