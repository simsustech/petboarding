# User Guide

Welcome to the Petboarding user guide. Whether you're a customer, employee, or administrator, you'll find everything you need to use Petboarding effectively.

## Who is this guide for?

Choose the guide that matches your role:

### [Customer Guide](/guide/customer)
For pet owners who use Petboarding to:
- Register their account and personal details
- Add pets, contact people, and manage their profile
- Create and manage boarding bookings
- Add daycare dates and purchase subscriptions
- Track booking and daycare status

### [Employee Guide](/guide/employee)
For staff members who manage daily operations:
- View daily arrivals, departures, and the agenda
- Assign pets to kennels with drag-and-drop
- Search and manage customers and pets
- Record vaccinations and pet relations
- Create bookings on behalf of customers
- Print labels and kennel layouts

### [Administrator Guide](/guide/administrator)
For managers who configure and oversee the facility:
- Approve, reject, or standby bookings and daycare dates
- Manage user accounts and assign roles
- Configure buildings, kennels, pricing, and opening times
- Set up services, subscriptions, and blocked periods
- Monitor occupancy and financials
- Post announcements and edit email templates

---

## Quick Start

1. Head to your facility's Petboarding URL (e.g. `https://demo.petboarding.app`)
2. Click **Login** and **Create account** to register
3. Complete your profile, add your pets, and you're good to go!

> **Demo access:** Visit [demo.petboarding.app](https://demo.petboarding.app) to explore Petboarding with sample data.
