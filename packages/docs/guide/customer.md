# Customer Guide

Welcome! This guide walks you through everything you can do as a customer — registering your account, adding your pets, making bookings, and handling daycare.

---

## Getting Started

### Registration

1. Go to your facility's Petboarding website
2. Click **Login** in the top-right corner
3. Click **Create account**
4. Fill in your email address and choose a password
5. Verify your email if required

### Navigation

Petboarding uses a sidebar menu. Click the menu button (☰) in the top-left corner to open it. The menu adapts to your role — as a customer, you'll see links for your account, bookings, and daycare.

---

## Setting Up Your Profile

Before you can make bookings or add daycare dates, you need to complete your profile.

### Customer Details

1. Open the sidebar menu and go to **Account → Customer details**
2. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) at the top
3. Fill in your personal information:
   - Gender, first name, last name
   - Address, postal code, city
   - Telephone number
   - Veterinarian (optional)
   - Comments (optional)
4. Click **Submit** to save

![Customer details page](/screenshots/customer-details.png)

### Contact People

Add emergency contacts — people the facility can reach if needed.

1. Go to **Account → Contact people**
2. Click the **Add** button (+) at the top
3. Enter the contact's first name, last name, and telephone number
4. Click **Submit**
5. Repeat to add more contacts

### Pets

Register every pet that will stay at the facility.

1. Go to **Account → Pets**
2. Click the **Add** button (+) at the top
3. Fill in your pet's details:
   - **Species** — Dog or cat
   - **Name**, breed, gender, birth date
   - **Color**, weight, chip number (if available)
   - **Sterilized** — Check if applicable
   - **Food** — Kind, amount per meal, and times per day
   - **Medicines** — Any medications your pet needs
   - **Comments** — Special instructions or particularities
   - **Photo** — Upload a picture of your pet
4. Click **Submit**
5. Repeat for each pet

![Pets overview](/screenshots/customer-pets.png)
5. Repeat for each pet

> **Tip:** Make sure your pet's vaccinations are up to date. The facility may require proof before approving bookings.

## Making a Boarding Booking

Book a stay for your pet at the facility.

1. Go to **Account → Bookings**
2. Click the **Add** button (+) at the top
3. **Select dates** — Click the start date and end date on the calendar
4. **Select times** — Choose your arrival and departure time slots
5. **Select pets** — Check the pets that will be staying
6. **Optional services** — Add services like washing or grooming if available
7. **Terms & conditions** — Check the box to agree
8. Click **Submit**

![Customer bookings page](/screenshots/customer-bookings.png)

### What happens next?

Your booking is now **pending**. An administrator will review it. You'll receive an email when it's:
- **Approved** — Your booking is confirmed. If a down payment is required, you'll receive payment instructions.
- **Rejected** — The booking cannot be accommodated.
- **Standby** — The facility is full, but you're on the waiting list.

### Managing bookings

- **View status** — Check your bookings page to see the current status of each booking
- **Modify** — You can edit a pending booking before it's reviewed
- **Cancel** — Cancel a booking if your plans change (cancelation fees may apply)

## Daycare

### Purchasing a Daycare Subscription

If your facility requires daycare subscriptions:

1. Go to **Account → Daycare**
2. Click **Purchase subscription**
3. Choose the subscription package that suits you
4. Complete the payment
5. Your subscription is now active — remaining days are shown on the daycare page

### Adding Daycare Dates

1. Go to **Account → Daycare**
2. Click the **Add** button (+) at the top
3. Select the dates your pet will attend on the calendar
4. Select which pets will attend
5. Click **Submit**

### Tracking Daycare Status

- Dates show as **pending** until reviewed by an administrator
- Once approved, they appear as **approved**
- You can cancel future daycare dates if your plans change

> **Note:** Daycare dates do not generate email confirmations. Check the daycare page to see their status.

---

## Changing Your Information

You can update your profile, pets, and contacts at any time:

- **Customer details** — Edit and resubmit your personal information
- **Contact people** — Add, edit, or remove emergency contacts
- **Pets** — Update pet details, food schedules, or upload new photos

---

## Tips

- **Keep vaccinations current** — Expired vaccinations may prevent booking approval
- **Book early** — Popular dates fill up quickly, especially during holidays
- **Check the availability page** — See which dates are blocked before planning
- **Read announcements** — Important facility updates appear on the home page
